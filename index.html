What We Handle

Three disciplines, one point of contact

No hand-offs between departments: the person who answers your call is the person doing the work.

01

Management

Coordination with agents and casting offices, scheduling built around tape deadlines and callbacks, and a standing view of where your career is headed over the next year, not just the next audition.

02

Production

We develop and produce original film projects for our roster, packaging talent with material, connecting financing, and creating opportunities that don't start with waiting on a casting call.

03

Social Media

Your dedicated social media manager is backed by agentic tools that monitor your accounts around the clock, surface relevant moments, and draft content at a pace no single person can sustain manually, extending their reach without replacing their judgment. Every piece of content is reviewed and edited by your manager before it goes out, so the presence stays authentically yours while the scope of what we can cover expands well beyond a typical nine-to-five.

The Approach

Built for a small roster, on purpose

We built Noctem for actors who are done being one of forty names on a roster, waiting on a callback from an assistant's assistant.

Every client works directly with the same person from the first read through the final signature, including at 11pm the night before a table read, because that is when this industry actually happens.

Client rosters stay deliberately small as we grow

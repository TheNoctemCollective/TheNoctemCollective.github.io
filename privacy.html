What Noctem Social is

Noctem Social helps talent research social media trends, draft captions, and publish posts to their own connected accounts. Draft captions and trend/news research are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic — not by a person. Nothing generated by Claude posts anywhere on its own: every draft sits in a review queue until the account holder reads it and taps "Post Now" themselves.

Information we collect

We collect only what each feature actually needs to work:

  • Account credentials — your email address and password, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase); we never see your password in plain text.
  • Profile information — a display name, and four voice-preference values (formality, energy, humor, directness) you set to control the tone Claude writes in. Both are optional and editable anytime.
  • Connected-platform status — which social platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Threads) you've connected, and an opaque reference token our posting provider (Ayrshare) uses to identify your connection. We do not store your Instagram/TikTok/Threads password.
  • Drafts and personal notes — captions, hashtags, and any real personal details you choose to type in when asking Claude to tailor a draft around your own voice or a piece of industry news.
  • Performance metrics — aggregated follower, engagement, and reach numbers pulled from your connected platforms once a day, so your Dashboard can show real trends over time.
  • Photos or videos you choose to attach to a post before publishing it.

What we don't collect

The Noctem Social app doesn't use browser cookies, location tracking, device fingerprinting, or any third-party analytics SDK — confirmed against the app's own code, not just stated. This website currently serves static content only and sets no cookies either; see our Cookie Policy for what that means and when it might change.

Who we share it with

We work with four named service providers. None of them receive more than what their part of the app actually needs:

Anthropic (Claude) Trend/news research topics, your voice-preference values, any personal notes you type in, and your existing caption as a style reference Researching trends and writing draft captions

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone outside this list.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. Draft captions you never post or schedule are automatically deleted after 7 days (you'll see a warning first). If you delete your account, everything tied to it — connected-platform links, synced metrics, drafts, and posting history — is permanently deleted at the same time. This does not remove posts already published to Instagram, TikTok, or Threads themselves; those are controlled by you directly on those platforms.

  • Delete your account — a real, immediate deletion built into the app (Profile → Delete Account), not just a request we process manually.
  • Ask us a question about what we hold on you by emailing TheNoctemCollective@proton.me.

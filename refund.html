Refund Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026
Forward-looking, not active billing terms. Noctem Social does not charge for access today, so there is no subscription to refund. This policy describes the terms that will apply once a paid subscription launches, written now so users know what to expect and so we don't scramble to write fair terms under launch pressure. Fields marked like this are real business decisions that need to be made (and this page updated) before any billing goes live. They aren't filled in here, since guessing at pricing/trial terms would just create a different policy than whatever actually ships.

1. No charges today

As of this writing, every feature of Noctem Social is free to use. Nothing in this policy is currently being applied to any real charge, because no charges exist yet.

2. Free trials, if introduced

If Noctem Social ever offers a free trial that automatically converts into a paid subscription, we will send you a reminder, by email or in-app notification, before you're charged, with enough notice to cancel if you don't want to continue. You will never be silently charged at the end of a trial with no warning.

3. Cancellation

Canceling a paid subscription will take no more steps than starting one did. You'll be able to cancel directly from your Profile in the app: no phone call, no support ticket required. Canceling stops future billing; it does not retroactively refund charges already made except as described below.

4. Refund window

We intend to offer a [to be decided, e.g., a 14-day] money-back window from the date of a new subscription charge: request a refund within that window for any reason and we'll issue it in full. Outside that window, subscription charges are generally non-refundable, except:

  • If we fail to deliver the service you paid for due to an error on our end;
  • If required by the law of your jurisdiction; or
  • At our discretion, on a case-by-case basis.

Renewal charges (after the first billing cycle) are handled the same way: request within the refund window, and we'll issue it.

5. Partial periods

If you cancel mid-billing-cycle, you'll keep access through the end of the period you already paid for; we don't currently plan to prorate refunds for unused time within a cycle outside the refund window above. [to be decided: confirm this is the intended policy once real pricing exists].

6. How to request a refund

Email TheNoctemCollective@proton.me with your account email and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within a few business days.

7. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page with real, final terms before any paid subscription launches, and note the change date at the top of this page.
